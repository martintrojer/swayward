`visible: false` at every depth. Sway arranges only the active child of a
tabbed or stacked container and sends the rest to `disable_container`
(`sway/desktop/transaction.c:316-321`), and `view_is_visible` checks the active
tiling child at every tabbed or stacked ancestor (`sway/tree/view.c:1180-1193`).
Previously every strip in the tree was drawn, so with the outer tab of a
three-level stack focused the two hidden inner strips covered the top of its
window.
