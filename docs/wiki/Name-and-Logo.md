**swayward** is written in lower case except where sentence capitalization
requires otherwise. The name is sway plus *-ward*: moving in the direction of
sway. It is also the ordinary English word for refusing to stay on the expected
path, which saved us from having to choose between accuracy and a pun.

The name describes the project without pretending the family tree is simple.
swayward keeps sway's protocol and i3's model, takes niri's foundation, then
heads off with the confidence of a fork that has seen a map and formed an
opinion about it.

The inherited logo assets are placeholders and retain their original licences.
They are not a final swayward identity. A project named after wandering away
has, fittingly, not yet arrived at a logo. It is taking the scenic route.
